#4: Arc Raiders

Arc Raiders earned its spot because the sci-fi setting and robot enemies look amazing. Fending off massive mechanical threats requires creative teamwork and quick thinking. The atmosphere is intense, making every successful extraction feel like a massive victory.

  • It is a cooperative extraction shooter developed by [Embark Studios](https://www.embark-studios.com/).
  • Players fight against a relentless mechanical threat known as the ARC.
  • The game was built using the advanced Unreal Engine 5 system.
