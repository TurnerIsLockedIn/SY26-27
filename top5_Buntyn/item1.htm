#1: Fortnite

Fortnite is my absolute favorite game because the building mechanics make it completely different from any other shooter. The constant updates and seasonal events always bring fresh content that keeps the gameplay from getting boring. Playing battle royale matches with my friends makes for some of the funniest and most intense gaming moments.

  • It features a unique 100-player Battle Royale mode.
  • The game was developed and published by Epic Games.
  • It includes pop culture crossovers ranging from Marvel characters to live music concerts.
