#2: Rainbow 6 Siege

Rainbow 6 Siege made my list because it requires a lot of strategy and communication instead of just running and shooting. Every round is tense since the walls can be destroyed, meaning enemies can attack from literally anywhere. Learning how to master the different operators and their gadgets keeps the game rewarding.

  • It is a tactical first-person shooter developed by Ubisoft.
  • The gameplay focuses heavily on environmental destruction and team cooperation.
  • Players choose from dozens of specialized counter-terrorism operators.
