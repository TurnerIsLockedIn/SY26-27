#5: Minecraft

Minecraft takes the final spot because it gives me total creative freedom to build whatever I want. Whether I am surviving the night in a sandbox world or starting massive building projects, the game never gets old. It is the perfect game to sit back, relax, and listen to the music while playing.

  • It is the best-selling video game of all time.
  • The game was originally created by Markus "Notch" Persson and developed by Mojang.
  • It features both a Survival mode and a completely open Creative mode.
